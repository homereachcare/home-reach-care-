💪 How We Can Support You - Our Services

Home-based services are arranged according to an individual assessment, clinical needs and the scope of the attending professional.


1. Physiotherapy at Home
Assessment and support for pain, stiffness, reduced mobility and muscle weakness. Care may include guided exercises, mobility training, education and a home exercise programme. We come to your home in Entebbe-Katabi, assess how you move, and create a plan that fits your daily life, your home space and your goals. Perfect if you find it hard to travel to a clinic.

2. Back Pain Support
Assessment of back discomfort and movement limitations, with suitable advice and exercises. Your plan will depend on the cause of your symptoms and clinical findings. We help you understand why your back hurts, teach you safe movements, posture and gentle exercises to reduce pain and prevent it from coming back. We also advise when you need to see a doctor.

3. Sciatica Assessment and Care
Support for symptoms such as pain travelling from the lower back into the leg, tingling or numbness. We assess your movement, strength and flexibility, and discuss appropriate management and referral when needed. You will learn exercises to relieve pressure on the nerve, safe sitting and walking habits, and what to avoid.

4. Stroke Rehabilitation
Rehabilitation support focused on mobility, balance, strength and everyday activities like walking, dressing and bathing. We can also guide family members and caregivers on safe support at home. Recovery takes time and patience. We work step-by-step with you to regain independence, improve confidence and prevent falls.

5. Home Nursing and Elderly Care
Ask us about nursing visits and support for older adults, including basic health checks, wound-care needs, mobility assistance and caregiver guidance. Specific procedures are subject to assessment and professional scope. We treat your loved ones with respect, dignity and kindness, and keep family informed.

6. Skin Therapy
Personalised skin-care consultations and suitable non-invasive skin treatments. We can discuss your concerns like acne, dark spots, dry skin or uneven tone, and advise when a medical review by an appropriate clinician is needed. We help you build a simple, safe skin routine that works for your skin type.

7. Therapeutic Massage
Relaxation and soft-tissue massage tailored to your comfort and needs. We discuss your health history first and avoid techniques that are not appropriate for your condition. Massage helps reduce muscle tension, stress, stiffness and improves blood circulation and sleep. You can enjoy it in your own home.

8. Cupping Therapy - Wet and Dry
Ask about dry cupping and wet cupping. Wet cupping involves skin puncture and blood exposure, so it requires strict infection-control precautions, appropriate training and careful screening. Suitability is assessed before treatment. Many people use cupping for back pain, muscle tightness and relaxation. We explain everything first and only proceed if it is safe for you.

9. Accident Rehabilitation and Walking Recovery
Rehabilitation support after road or other accidents, including mobility exercises, balance practice, strengthening and supported walking retraining when clinically appropriate. We coordinate with your treating clinician where needed. We help you walk again with confidence, step by step, safely at home.
